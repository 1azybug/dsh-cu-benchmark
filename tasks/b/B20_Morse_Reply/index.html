It's a stormy night at sea. You are the ship's radio operator — headquarters calls, and you must reply R (· − ·, "received / Roger") on the telegraph key. Press and hold the key (or hold the SPACE bar): a short hold of ≤0.2s sends a dot (·), a long hold of 0.2~0.5s sends a dash (−), holding over 0.5s is too long. Three holds total: short – long – short.
海上风雨夜，你是船上的无线电操作员——总部呼叫，你要用发报键回复摩斯字母 R（· − ·，代表「收到」）。按住发报键（或按住空格键）：短按 ≤0.2 秒 发点（·），长按 0.2~0.5 秒 发划（−），按住超过 0.5 秒 则太长。共按 3 次：短 – 长 – 短。
