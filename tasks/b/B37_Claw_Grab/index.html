Claw Grab 摆锤下抓
In the gold mine, a retracted claw hangs from the ceiling and swings like a pendulum from the left side with a period of 2 seconds per cycle. A gold nugget sits at a random position below (always visible). Press SPACE when the claw swings directly over the gold — the claw then extends, grabs the gold, and pulls back. Read the gold's position + the 2s period + the live angle to time your grab.
在金矿里，一只缩着的抓斗从左侧像单摆一样荡下，周期为 每循环 2 秒。一块金块躺在下方随机位置（始终可见）。当抓斗摆到金块正上方时按 空格下抓——抓斗会伸出、抓住金块、收回。读金块位置 + 2 秒周期 + 实时角度，即可预判何时下抓。
