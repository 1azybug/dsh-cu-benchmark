Combo Sequence 连招序列
In this fighting game, input the 4-direction combo → ↓ → ↑ (arrow keys in order) when the countdown reaches 0.00 (within ±0.75s). Your fighter performs one move per correct key — press all 4 in order to land the finishing combo. A wrong key or bad timing breaks the combo.
在这款格斗游戏里，当倒计时走到 0.00 时（±0.75 秒内）按顺序输入 4 方向连招 → ↓ → ↑（方向键按序）。你的格斗家每按对一个键就打出一招——4 个键全按对即可打出终结连击。按错键或时机不对都会断连。
