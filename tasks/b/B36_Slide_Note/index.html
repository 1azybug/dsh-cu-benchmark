Slide Note 滑动到尾端
In this rhythm game, drag the note knob along the straight track from START to the green END zone within 2 seconds. The countdown shows the remaining time — finish (release the knob at END) before it reaches 0.00. Dragging the knob off the track fails, and running out of time fails.
在这款音游里，沿直线滑轨把音符滑块从起点拖到绿色尾端 END区，须在 2 秒内完成。倒计时显示剩余时间——在它走到 0.00 之前完成（在 END 松手）。拖出轨道会失败，超时也会失败。
